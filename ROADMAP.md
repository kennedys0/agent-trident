# Roadmap

1. Foundation: monorepo, API/web skeletons, migrations, health checks, design tokens.
2. Vertical slice: verified WETH/USDG snapshot, deterministic WAIT/BUY evaluation, risk gate, paper order, ledger, UI.
3. Reliability: checkpoints, restart recovery, replay/idempotency, stale/RPC fault injection, audit exports.
4. Product expansion: scanner/watchlist, strategy configuration, positions/trades, risk center, operations and settings.
5. Live readiness only: signer isolation and deployment design after a separate approval. Activation remains prohibited.
